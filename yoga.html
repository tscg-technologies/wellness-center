Foundational Postures

Hatha Yoga

A classic, steady-paced practice focusing on structural alignment, conscious physical poise, and breath retention within each posture.

  • Suitable Experience Level: All levels, from beginners to experienced practitioners
  • Typical Duration: 60 minutes
  • What to expect: Clear posture cues, gentle holds (3 to 6 breaths per posture), and guided final relaxation.

Fluid Dynamics

Vinyasa Yoga

A seamless, breath-synchronized sequence linking posture to posture with fluid motion, gently building internal warmth and cardiovascular endurance.

  • Suitable Experience Level: Intermediate or active beginners
  • Typical Duration: 60 minutes
  • What to expect: Continuous rhythmic transitions, sun salutation variations, and invigorating balance postures.

Soft & Nurturing

Gentle Yoga

Slow, compassionate movements emphasizing joint mobility, spinal decompression, and effortless breath flow without strain or high exertion.

  • Suitable Experience Level: All levels, ideal for seniors and recovery phases
  • Typical Duration: 45 to 50 minutes
  • What to expect: Seated and reclined postures, gentle hip openers, and zero aggressive transitions.

Deep Surrender

Restorative Yoga

Passive postures supported fully by props (bolsters, folded blankets, blocks) allowing muscles to relax deeply and autonomic arousal to subside.

  • Suitable Experience Level: Everyone; specifically beneficial during fatigue or stress
  • Typical Duration: 60 to 75 minutes
  • What to expect: 4 to 6 long-held supported postures (5–12 minutes each) in a softly lit, warm environment.

Beginner Yoga

  • Suitable Experience Level: First-time practitioners
  • Typical Duration: 50 minutes
  • What to expect: Clear anatomical cues, props demonstrations, pace for asking questions, and no prior flexibility needed.

Functional Freedom

Stretching and Mobility

Targeted myofascial release, joint articulation, and functional active stretching aimed at restoring healthy range of motion in the hips, shoulders, and spine.

  • Suitable Experience Level: All levels, great for runners, desk workers, and athletes
  • Typical Duration: 45 minutes
  • What to expect: Controlled articular rotations, gentle resistance band work, and targeted posture opening.
