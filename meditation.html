Daily Takeaway

A Simple 5-Minute Daily Routine

You can begin cultivating presence right now with this gentle four-step practice.

Step 1: Find a Comfortable Seat (1 minute)

Sit upright on a supportive chair or cushion. Rest your hands on your thighs. Let your spine be comfortably tall without rigid strain, and soften your eyes or gently close them.

Step 2: Ground Through the Body (1 minute)

Feel the contact of your feet on the floor and your weight on the seat. Release tension in your jaw, drop your shoulders away from your ears, and soften the belly.

Step 3: Anchor to Natural Breath (2 minutes)

Notice the natural breath moving in and out through your nose. Do not force or deepen it artificially. Simply observe the rise and fall of your abdomen.

Step 4: Notice and Gently Return (1 minute)

When you notice your mind wandering to memories or to-do lists, silently acknowledge: "thinking", and kindly escort your attention back to your next breath.
